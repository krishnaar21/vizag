    Carry – Water, snacks, tea flask; stalls at the top may be shut

    Go by, best first
    1. 🥾 Trek

    Go by, best first
    1. 🚙 Same cab
    Carry – Car snacks; few stalls until Borra
  5. 🌉11:15

    Wooden bridge, Sunkarametta

    Go by, best first
    1. 🚙 Same cab
    2. 🚶 Walk
  6. ⛪Skip if late12:00

    CBM Church, Sunkarimetta

    Short stop near the wooden bridge, about 20 minutes.

    Go by, best first
    1. 🚙 Same cab
  7. 🍗12:45

    Lunch near Borra

    Go by, best first
    1. 🚙 Same cab
    Food – ₹600–1,000
  8. 🪨13:30

    Borra Caves

    2. 🚶 Walk
    Entry – ₹60 eachPhone camera – ₹25
  9. 💦Skip if late15:00

    Go by, best first
    1. 🛻 Local jeep
    2. 🥾 Trek
    Jeep – ask at BorraCarry – Water & snacks; nothing on the trail
  10. 🚕17:30

    Drive to Vizag

    About 88 km of ghat road, 2.5–3 hours.

    Go by, best first
    1. 🚙 Same cab
    Carry – Snacks in case of delays
  11. 🧳20:30

    Bags from VSKP, check in, dinner

    Show the cloakroom receipt, then the hotel around Dwaraka Nagar.

    Go by, best first
    1. 🚙 Same cab
    2. 🛺 Auto
    Food – ₹900–1,500

  • Longest day, 03:15 to about 21:00. If everyone is tired after Borra, drop Katiki and reach Vizag by about 19:00.
  • Borra Caves are damp and slippery inside. Wear shoes with grip.
  • Try to finish the ghat road before it gets fully dark.
  • Keep the cloakroom receipt handy for the 20:30 bag pickup.
